# Color

Blue anchors project titles, links and the interface. Orange provides a clear accent, including the underline beneath headings and primary icon actions. Use neutrals for page backgrounds, surfaces, borders and body text. Do not add colored accent lines to the left or top edges of cards, panels or other blocks.

These colors are useful starting points; adapt them to the context and check actual combinations in both themes.

| Role | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page | `#f3f6fc` | `#0c1729` |
| Surface | `#ffffff` | `#14243c` |
| Body text | `#182b49` | `#edf3ff` |
| Muted text | `#50617c` | `#b2c2da` |
| Border | `#cfdaeb` | `#354b6b` |
| Blue links and titles | `#1558ba` | `#8dbbff` |
| Orange accent | `#a83e00` | `#ffb16d` |
| Orange fill | `#ff932e` | `#ff932e` |

Use sufficient contrast for text, controls and focus indicators against their actual backgrounds. Dark ink is appropriate on the orange fill; white small text on light orange is generally too weak. Distinguish status through labels or other cues as well as color. Preserve meaningful application data colors and their semantics.
