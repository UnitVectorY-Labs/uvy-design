Sidebar and navigation

The same 2px signal-orange rule marks the active location across header links, sidebar items and tabs. Below 768px, the sidebar becomes an accessible drawer.

Use left-rail navigation for catalogs and documentation with many destinations. Keep search sticky above long lists and omit filters on short menus.

Use ArrowLeft and ArrowRight to move across tabs. Inside the mobile drawer, Tab wraps within the panel and Escape restores focus to the menu trigger.

Resize below 768px to test the slide-over drawer. Background landmarks become inert while the drawer is open and unlock immediately on dismissal or resize to desktop.
